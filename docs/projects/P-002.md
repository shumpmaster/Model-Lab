## 2. Strategy
*In plain language: the chain from the deliverables to the value — how this work produces it.*

*Draft by the Chief of Staff, 2026-11-09, for the Critic's check. The chain from the work to the value follows once the value target is ruled.*

**The problem, stated without reference to any industry (Discovery-4).** One person supervises a team of delegated workers who do most of the planning and making. The person wants a larger share of that work carried by the workers from start to finish, with the person trusting what comes back and the work holding up when it is used. The methods for organising such teams change quickly. Most are reported by people who sell, promote or simply like them, few are tested by anyone independent, and a claimed gain can shrink or vanish when someone else tries it. Waiting for a method to be fully proven means adopting it after it has stopped being new. The problem is to find promising methods early, try each on the team's own work in a small trial that can show whether it helps here, keep what helps, and retire what does not or no longer does, without taking on more change than the person can check, letting harm in through what is read, or mistaking a rise in the share of work done for a rise in work that holds up.

**What other fields found, and what it may mean here.** Each point cites the portfolio library entry or the source-checked memo it rests on. Most rest on one source, and the "may mean here" lines are the Chief of Staff's reading, to be tested, not findings.
- *Claims shrink when someone else tests them.* In software-engineering replications, 26% of external replications conformed to the original study, against 82% of internal ones; this is a secondary citation (library Q-011-1). An independent replication of five popular prompting techniques across six models found a general lack of statistically significant differences (Q-011-4). *May mean here:* treat a reported gain as a ceiling, and judge a method only by our own trial.
- *Looking outside helps up to a point.* In 2,707 UK manufacturing firms, searching widely and deeply related to innovation performance in an inverted U, with decreasing returns (Q-011-11). No study compares looking with not looking in AI work (research/Q-011-memo.md, answer). *May mean here:* a few well-chosen feeds, not every source.
- *What is borrowed can carry harm.* Of 1.3 million Android apps, 15.4% contained security code copied from Stack Overflow, and 97.9% of those had at least one insecure snippet (Q-011-10). Scanners of one agent-skill ecosystem labelled up to 46.8% of skills malicious, against 0.52% after context-aware analysis; the two measure different things (Q-011-19). *May mean here:* the owner's "no data out" rule stands, and outside code, prompts and skills are checked before they run.
- *Findings about AI models go stale quickly.* Two models' behaviour changed substantially between March and June 2023 (Q-012-3); gains from structured prompting shrank or turned negative on newer models of one family in one study (Q-012-4). *May mean here:* every kept method carries a review date, and a model change triggers a re-check.
- *Small trials see only large effects.* A paired trial of 10 runs detects about one standard deviation of the run-to-run differences, and 20 runs about two-thirds of one, on the memo's own calculation; below that, significant results often overstate the effect, and a plan changed mid-trial or looked at early raises false alarms (research/Q-013-memo.md, answer). *May mean here:* trial only methods that promise a large gain, fix the plan first, and report a smaller gain as "not shown", not as "no effect".
- *Whether a method transfers is hard to predict in advance.* No conditions checklist was found that was applied before a practice moved and then scored against what happened, in any field (research/Q-010-memo.md, short answer). *May mean here:* the trial on our own work decides, not a checklist.
- *Retiring is hard, and scanning fades without a process.* Of 7 low-value medical services flagged by one campaign, 2 declined, 3 stayed flat and 2 rose (Q-014-13); active change work was associated with the greatest likelihood of de-adoption (Q-014-12). Zalando reports its first technology radar was easy to ignore without a formal process (Q-014-18, self-report). Two audits called for time limits on temporary changes and routine review of all open ones (Q-014-4). *May mean here:* a plain routine with a set rhythm, and retirement by default at each review unless a method has shown value.
- *Cheap alerts miss a lot.* In one test, each of two cheap alerts found 1 of the 4 new trials a full search found (Q-012-15); two threat-intelligence feeds overlapped by 2.5–4.0% (Q-012-17). *May mean here:* more than one feed, and a periodic fuller look, with what each finds counted.
