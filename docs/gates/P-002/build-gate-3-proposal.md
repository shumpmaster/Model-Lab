# P-002 — the Build gate: what a user would see

**Shown to the user representative, a simulation of the target user, not real users.**

## 1. What it is

A standing screening of new ways of running a team of AI agents, from chosen feeds to a fair trial of each method the owner lets through [docs/projects/P-002.md:277].
It counts how often the owner has to step in on a piece of work before it merges, by written rules first tried by hand on past work [docs/projects/P-002.md:136].

## 2. What it does for you

The aim is to halve the share of work that needs the owner to step in, with the work holding up, checked 12 months after the first trial [docs/projects/P-002.md:192].
Each method comes first on an entry card the owner approves, and its result on a results card the owner rules; nothing enters the operating model without the owner's ruling [docs/projects/P-002.md:138].

## 4. What it looks like

No screens: the screening has no screens of its own; the owner sees cards, status updates and the desk's existing views, and nothing new is drawn for it [docs/projects/P-002.md:124].

## 5. What it asks of you

AI usage within the owner's existing plan, with no paid services, data or tools [docs/projects/P-002.md:175].
The owner's time, with no cap: two cards a trial and five short reads a look [docs/projects/P-002.md:197] [docs/projects/P-002.md:176].
