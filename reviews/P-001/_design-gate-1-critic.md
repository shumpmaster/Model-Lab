## The Critic's independent view

I read the case, the gate form, docs/PROJECT.md and every record the case cites, except for the Q-001 memo and the Q-002 to Q-007 corrections confirmation. For those two I relied on the Q-008 memo's own references to them. The facts hold up. The brief passed its readiness check at round 2, and the Q-008 source check ended at PASS. The three owner rulings on the value target, the open unknowns and "go on now" are on record. The spend ceiling, the 100-run budget and the no-change result are also on record. Four things need fixing, none of them about the substance.

- **Unsupported claim.** Section 6 says "none used yet" for the test runs, and no record says it.
- **Likely over length.** My own count puts sections 1 to 8 at roughly 370 words, over the form's 350, but I did not check this against a tool.
- **Missing omissions.** The case leaves out two facts a reader weighing the ask would want. The owner's own PROJECT.md section 6 puts the full test at about 300 runs for the three kinds counted so far. It also says the V2 miss rule removes a kept change that falls short at the model's next scheduled review of its rules.
- **Missing visuals.** Only the six-phase visual is drawn. The form's standard visuals for sections 2, 3, 4, 6 and 9 are absent. The owner-time caps visual would be a plain "no cap" and the research-outcomes visual would be the Q-008 figures, so neither adds a number the records lack.

**The Critic's recommendation:** proceed — the brief passed its definition-of-ready check, and the starting figures passed their source check. The owner has already ruled "go on now", and has accepted U2, U3 and U4 as open into Design. The fair test is the next work, and its size and budget come back to the owner at the Build gate. The issues above should be fixed in the case before it goes to the owner, but none of them changes the recommendation.
