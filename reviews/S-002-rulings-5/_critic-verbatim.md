On the Definer's three questions: the desk record is a limit rightly stated and work rightly given to another lane, with two small things to add; the two rules for withdrawn and settled pieces hang together, but each leaves the owner's correction without effect on the count in a case the side-knowing roles can bring about, and that should be said in the plain words and the limits lists, and the result given both ways.
