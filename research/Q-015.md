# Research question — Q-015

## The question

How are systems of several AI agents, working with one or a few human supervisors, organised, run and improved in practice for planning, strategy and software work, and what is reported about each way of doing it? Cover, evenly: how work is divided between agents and between agents and people; how much each agent may do without a person's approval, and how that is decided and changed; how work is handed from one agent to the next; how agents' work is checked, by other agents, by tests or by people; how the instructions, tools and memory agents work with are kept and changed; how failures are found, recorded and fixed; and how such systems are changed over time. For each, report who reports it (a vendor, a practitioner, an academic study or an independent test), on what kind of work, at what scale and with how much human oversight, what was claimed and what was measured, and any case where it was found no better than a simpler setup, or worse. Include methods reported only by their makers, marked as such; a method's being new or untested is not a reason to leave it out. Where breadth and depth conflict, cover every area, even thinly.

Facts already in the portfolio library (/home/user/portfolio/library/, index in README.md) need not be re-researched; in particular the Q-002, Q-009 and Q-010 to Q-014 entries.

## The decision it serves

Which ways of running and improving a system of AI agents are candidates to try on the organisation's own work, and what each one's reported conditions are.

## What each answer would change

Each way found with its conditions becomes a candidate for a trial, or is set aside if its conditions clearly do not hold; areas where nothing is reported become gaps to say plainly; ways found no better than a simpler setup are recorded so they are not tried blind.
