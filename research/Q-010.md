# Research question — Q-010

## The question

What methods exist for judging whether a practice reported for building software and products with AI models and agents will hold in a different setting, for example a different team size, model, kind of product or degree of human oversight? How well have those methods predicted transfer: where a method was applied before a practice was carried to a new setting, how often did its prediction match what then happened there?

This question owns methods for predicting transfer; how proposals are judged overall is a separate question. Cover methods from any field that has studied whether findings carry between settings, and report where they predicted well, where they did not, and where no test of their predictions exists. Facts already in the portfolio library (/home/user/portfolio/library/, index in README.md) need not be re-researched: entries Q-009-F6-1 and Q-009-F6-3 to F6-6.

## The decision it serves

Whether a check of a practice's conditions is applied before an outside practice is adopted, and if so, which.

## What each answer would change

A method shown to predict transfer well becomes a candidate check; a method shown to predict poorly weighs against using it; a method that predicts well only in some settings is a candidate for those settings; if no method has been tested, the choice of check rests on other grounds.
