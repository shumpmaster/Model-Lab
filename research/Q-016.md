# Research question — Q-016

## The question

How do organisations and researchers measure how much of a body of work AI agents carry, how far people trust that work, and whether it holds up in use? Cover, evenly: measures of the share of work done by AI (by task, by time, by output such as code or documents, or by cost), and how each is counted; measures of human trust and reliance, including how often people review, correct, override or redo agents' work, and measures of misplaced trust in either direction; measures of whether the work holds up later (defects, rework, reversals, failures in use); and how these are combined or traded off. For each measure, report who uses it, on what kind of work, how it is collected, how it can be gamed or misread, and what is known about how well it tracks the outcome it stands for. Include published figures for the share of work done by AI in named organisations, with what each figure counts and who reported it. Where breadth and depth conflict, cover every area, even thinly.

Facts already in the portfolio library (/home/user/portfolio/library/, index in README.md) need not be re-researched; in particular the Q-002, Q-009 and Q-010 to Q-014 entries.

## The decision it serves

How the organisation measures the share of its work its agents carry, the trust in it and whether it holds up, so that a change can be shown to help or harm.

## What each answer would change

A measure that can be collected from the organisation's own records and is known to track its outcome becomes a candidate for the value target; a measure known to be easily gamed or misread is paired with a check or left out; a published share-of-work figure is used only with what it counts stated.
