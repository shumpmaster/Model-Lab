# Research question — Q-016

## The question

How do organisations and researchers measure how much of a body of work AI agents do, how far people rely on and trust that work, and whether it holds up later? Cover, evenly:
- measures of the share of work done by AI: by task, by time, by output such as code or documents, or by cost; how "the work" being divided is defined when people and AI both contribute; and how a person's and an agent's parts are told apart;
- measures of reliance and trust: how often people review, accept, correct, override or redo agents' work; how often AI output is kept, for example code that is merged and stays; and whether trust matches how often the work is right, in either direction;
- measures of whether work holds up: defects, rework, reversals and failures in use, and how long after the work they show;
- measures of time saved or value, and how they relate to share measures;
- how measures are collected (people's own reports, logs and telemetry, or review of the work), and what collecting them costs;
- measures for work other than code, such as plans, analyses and documents, and whether any are known;
- comparison with the same work done without AI.

For each measure, report who uses it, on what kind of work, how it can be gamed or misread, and what is known about how well it tracks what it is meant to show. Include published figures for the share of work done by AI in named organisations, with what each figure counts, who reported it and any stake they have, and whether anyone outside has checked it.

This question owns measuring the share of work, reliance, trust and whether work holds up, including review and override rates. The ways of running and checking agents belong to Q-015.

Facts already in the portfolio library (/home/user/portfolio/library/, index in README.md) need not be re-researched; in particular the entries from Q-005 (including alert override rates), Q-006 and Q-008 (one pipeline's own return and first-pass counts), and Q-009.

## The decision it serves

Which measures, if any, one small organisation of AI agents supervised by one person could use to tell whether a change in how it works helped, did nothing or harmed.

## What each answer would change

A measure that can be collected from an organisation's own records and is known to track what it stands for becomes a measure to use; one known to be easily gamed or misread is paired with a check or left out; a published share-of-work figure is used only with what it counts, and its source's stake, stated.
