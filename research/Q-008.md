## The question

In the records of work done under the operating model, how often was each kind of checked work sent back by its check, and for what reasons, as far as the records allow?

1. **Counts.** For each kind of work that goes to an independent check (plans to their review, research to its source check, built work to its reviewer, and any other kind the records show, such as owner questions to the Critic or gate cases), give the number of pieces, how many passed at the first try, and how many rounds each took. Define "passed at the first try" before counting (for example, how a later-added check, a change of checker, or a piece with no recorded verdict is treated), and list per kind the pieces left out and why, including dropped work and gaps in the records.
2. **Every return.** Classify every return in the records, not a sample. If that is not feasible for a kind, draw a sample by a rule stated before looking at the returns (for example, every n-th return in date order), and give the sample's size and the total it was drawn from, per kind.
3. **Was it avoidable?** For each return, mark whether the record shows that it caught a real fault the work should not have had in it given what the worker was handed (avoidable), or that it was something the worker could not have avoided, or that the record cannot say. State the test you used.
4. **Cause.** For each return whose record shows a cause, name every contributing cause the record supports from this list, each judged on the same terms: the material handed over (missing, wrong or too much); the written instructions (the role file or the task's brief); how the work was split between agents or steps; the capability of the agent or its AI model; the check itself (wrong, inconsistent or changed); or something else, named. Then name one primary cause by a rule you state before classifying (for example, the cause whose correction alone would have prevented the return), and say how ties are handled. Where a brief failed to pass on material, say so under both causes and state which you made primary and why.
5. **What the records cannot show.** Say per kind how many returns have no recorded cause, and how many pieces have no check record at all.

Report every figure per kind and every cause on equal terms; whether any cause is large or small is not for this memo to judge.

Records to read: shumpmaster/Service-Desk (its dispatch log, reviews/, research/ and session records), shumpmaster/Model-Lab (its reviews/, research/ and the briefs in docs/briefs/), and shumpmaster/Personal-Org-Operating-Model's own reviews/. Earlier counts are in Model-Lab's research/Q-006-memo.md; re-derive every figure you use, and where yours differs, say which record decides it.

Out of scope: outside literature, and any recommendation of what to change.

## The decision it serves

The Discovery of P-001: the starting figure for each kind of work against which the owner's value target is measured (docs/PROJECT.md section 6), whether the records can show causes at all (section 7, U5), and which causes account for the avoidable returns in each kind of work (section 7, U1), which the owner uses under the project's stop rule.

## What each answer would change

The counts per kind replace the provisional starting figures in section 6. Where the records show causes, the causes found, whichever they are, decide where candidate changes are looked for: in the material handed over, the instructions, the split of work, the choice of model or the checks. Where the records cannot show causes, causes are measured from new work first. The owner decides, from the counts, whether the project goes on as aimed, is re-aimed at other causes, or closes.
