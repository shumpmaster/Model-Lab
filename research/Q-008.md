## The question

In the records of work done under the operating model, how often was each kind of checked work sent back by its check, and for what reasons, as far as the records allow? Count, for each kind of work that goes to an independent check (plans to their review, research to its source check, built work to its reviewer, and any other kind the records show, such as owner questions to the Critic or gate cases), how many pieces passed at the first try and how many rounds each took. For every return in a stated sample, say whether the record shows its cause, and if so classify it: the check catching a real fault in the work; missing or wrong material handed over; how the work was split; the written instructions (role file or brief); the capability of the agent or its model; the check itself being wrong or inconsistent; or something else, named. Say plainly where the records cannot show a cause, and how many returns that leaves.

Records to read: shumpmaster/Service-Desk (its dispatch log, reviews/, research/ and session records), shumpmaster/Model-Lab (its reviews/, research/ and the briefs in docs/briefs/), and shumpmaster/Personal-Org-Operating-Model's own reviews/. Earlier counts exist in research/Q-006-memo.md; re-derive every figure you use, and where yours differs, say which record decides it.

Out of scope: outside literature, and any recommendation of what to change.

## The decision it serves

The Discovery of P-001: the starting figure for each kind of work against which the owner's value target is measured (docs/PROJECT.md section 6, V2), whether the records can show causes at all (section 7, U5), and whether the instructions are a meaningful cause of avoidable returns for any kind of work (section 7, U1; section 6, stop rule).

## What each answer would change

Starting figures per kind replace the provisional ones in section 6. If the records show causes, candidate changes are chosen from the causes found; if they cannot, causes are measured from new work first. If the instructions cause few or none of the avoidable returns in every kind of work, the project goes back to the owner to re-aim or close.
