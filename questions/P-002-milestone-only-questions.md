# Ruling needed: what to do with a question whose record names only a milestone

Milestone: M8

Some questions you answer while a piece is under way are recorded with only a milestone, not the piece. Your Service-Desk answer on the board's scope is one example: it went into Service-Desk #148's work, but its record names only the milestone, so no counting rule ties it to #148, and the hand check misses that touch. From the start of the count, the touch log the Chief of Staff keeps by hand catches such touches. The choice is about the past count only. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the starting count can miss real touches of this kind; the plan does not change until you choose (specs/S-002.md, open question 14).
OPTIONS:
  A. Keep it missed, on record as a touch the rules cannot catch; the touch log catches it from the start of the count.
     Gain: no rule that counts touches on pieces that were not asked about.
     Cost: the starting figure undercounts this kind of touch.
  B. Add a reverse citation: a question record that the piece's own commits cite, inside its window, names the piece.
     Gain: it would likely catch the board-scope answer.
     Cost: it rests on a citation no one has yet checked #148 made, and it needs a new extract field.
  C. Count a milestone-only question on every piece of that milestone under way when it is answered.
     Gain: it never misses one.
     Cost: it counts touches on pieces that run side by side and were not asked about.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with moderate confidence (the Definer's and the Chief of Staff's judgement): the touch log closes the gap for every piece the count covers, and B and C each add an unchecked or over-counting rule. What would change it: the hand check showing many such misses (then B).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic's re-check of the corrected rules (reviews/Q-018-rules/_critic-verbatim.md, section 3(d)).
RISK CLASS: R0, a question only   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the hand check's touch count and the starting figure; not trials, which use the touch log
DEFAULT: none: the counting rules change only on your answer (specs/S-002.md, open question 14)   TIMEOUT: none
