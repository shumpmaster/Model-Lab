# Ruling needed: whether the rulings the model requires of you before the Design gate count as touches

Milestone: M8

Until you answer the plan's earlier question on gate and value-target rulings, the counting rules leave those rulings out of the count. They find them by what each ruling's own record says it is. Other rulings the operating model requires of you count as touches today. One example is accepting a project unknown as open before the Design gate. This card asks whether those readiness rulings should stay in, or be left out with the gate rulings. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the starting count of how often pieces need you rests on which rulings are left out; the plan does not change until you choose (specs/S-002.md, open question 13).
OPTIONS:
  A. Leave out only gate and value-target rulings, by what their records say; every other ruling counts, and each counted ruling tagged with a gate's or the value target's milestone is listed for you.
     Gain: it rests on what each record says it is, so it needs no new judgement.
     Cost: a readiness ruling you had to give counts as a touch on the pieces it rules.
  B. Leave out readiness rulings with the gates.
     Gain: required rulings are treated alike.
     Cost: no record classes itself as a readiness ruling, so the coder would have to read that from a citation, not from what the record says it is.
  C. Leave out every ruling tagged with a gate's or the value target's milestone, and list each one left out for you.
     Gain: one simple test.
     Cost: it leaves out rulings that say they are something else, such as your unasked ruling on the project's aim and a choice under the stop rule.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with fair confidence (the Definer's and the Chief of Staff's judgement): it is the only option that rests on the records alone, and the list shows you each borderline record. What would change it: ruling that every required ruling is outside what a method can reduce (then B).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic's re-checks of the corrected rules, which found the question's wording fair at the third check (reviews/Q-018-rules/_critic-r3-verbatim.md, brief item 3).
RISK CLASS: R0, a question only   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the hand check's touch count and the starting figure; every trial's count of touches
DEFAULT: none: the counting rules change only on your answer (specs/S-002.md, open question 13)   TIMEOUT: none
