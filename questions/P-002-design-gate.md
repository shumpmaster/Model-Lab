# Ruling needed: move the screening into Design, and set what it may spend, what it may ask of you, and the terms its trials start with

Milestone: Discovery-8, M7

WHY: The screening is the project that looks for better ways of running your team of agents, tries the promising ones on your own work, and brings each to you to keep or not. Its first phase, finding out, is done: its plan passed its last check, and the sums behind your target were re-run by someone who did not write them. Only you can move it into the next phase, where the counting, the trials and the reading of sources are written down before anything is counted or tried. With that, your earlier answers left some terms of the trials open, and the limits on spend and on your time were left to now. Until these are ruled, no trial can be planned. The case for going on is the gate document; the Critic's own view of it is beside it; every trial term, marked ruled by you or proposed, is in its list. Nothing here is irreversible except closing the project (Part 1, D). In ten parts, each ruled on its own.

**Words.** A **piece** is a merged pull request that changes built work, a plan or research. A **touch** is a time you stepped in on a piece before it merged (a question, a fix of yours, a send-back). A **trial** tries one method on 30 new pieces of one kind against 30 without, assigned by shuffled draws, with a planned **second look** of 30 more each way if the first leaned the right way. A **look** is a point where a trial is judged. The **entry card** is your approval before a trial; the **results card** is your decision after it. The **prompt engine** is this repository's first project, which is testing changes to how agents are briefed and counting first-time passes; it is in its building phase.
