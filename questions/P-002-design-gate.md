**Words.** A **piece** is a merged pull request that changes built work, a plan or research. A **touch** is a time you stepped in on a piece before it merged (a question, a fix of yours, a send-back). A **trial** tries one method on 30 new pieces of one kind against 30 without, assigned by shuffled draws, with a planned **second look** of 30 more each way. A **look** is a point where a trial is judged. A **short read** is you reading one of a trial's pieces at a look, chosen at random and unlabelled. The **entry card** is your approval before a trial; the **results card** is your decision after it. The **prompt engine** is this repository's first project, which is testing changes to how agents are briefed and counting how often work passes its first check; it is in its building phase. A **proposal** below is marked either *from your cards* (the two cards you answered) or *the Chief of Staff's own* (on neither card).
