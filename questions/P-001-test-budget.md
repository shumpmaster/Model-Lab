# Ruling needed: How much may this project spend on testing instruction changes?

Milestone: Discovery-3
Asked in session: session_01QBwHGJH9m3A4Ray7WpHzBo

WHY: Telling a real improvement from chance needs several runs of the same task, and each agent run costs usage. Reviewer runs in one Service-Desk session used between about 57,000 and 214,000 tokens each; most runs recorded no figure. Without a limit, the research cannot size its tests, and the rulebook leaves the research cost limit open.
OPTIONS:
  A. A run count — up to about 100 agent runs for testing, covering Discovery and Design (at the sizes seen, about 6 to 21 million tokens); a new limit is put to you at the Build gate. Cost: about 6 to 21 million tokens of your plan's usage if all are used.
  B. A money limit — a monthly figure you name, checked against your plan's usage. Cost: you must pick a figure now, before the research has sized what a test needs.
  C. No extra spend — use only records that already exist (past reviews and sessions), with no new test runs, until the Design gate. Cost: past records are incomplete (much conversation work has no recorded brief or model), so a fair before-and-after test may not be possible.
RECOMMENDATION: A — an estimate: a run count is simple to track, and about 100 runs should be enough to learn whether testing is workable before committing more; how many a firm answer needs is not yet known.
