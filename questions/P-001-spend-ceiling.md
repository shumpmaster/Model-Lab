# Ruling needed: the prompt engine's spend ceiling

Milestone: Discovery-7

WHY: Every project names the most it may spend before it leaves Discovery. Setting it is yours and cannot be delegated. Until you rule, the brief cannot go to the Design gate.

What is already ruled: up to about 100 test runs through Design, with token checkpoints; the budget for the test itself (about 300 runs for three kinds of work, more if the count finds further kinds) comes to you at the Build gate.

OPTIONS:
  A. **Nothing beyond your existing AI plan.** No paid services, data, tools or extra AI usage outside the plan; AI usage inside it is limited by your test-run rulings. *Gain:* no new money at risk. *Cost:* if the plan's weekly limit is reached, work waits for it to reset. *Reversible:* yes.
  B. **Your plan, plus up to a set amount for extra AI usage,** the amount named by you, if your plan offers paid usage beyond its limit. *Gain:* work need not wait for the limit to reset. *Cost:* real money, up to the amount you set. *Reversible:* the ceiling can be changed later; money spent is not returned.

RECOMMENDATION: A, with high confidence: nothing in the plan needs a paid service, and with no fixed timeline for the project, waiting for a reset costs little (the Chief of Staff's reading). The ceiling can be raised later if the test's budget at the Build gate needs it. What would change it: if you want the work never to wait on the weekly limit, B.
