# Ruling needed: is your approval of a plan about the piece whose own work wrote that plan, when the piece is labelled with another plan?

Milestone: M8

A question that asks for your approval counts against the piece it is about. In the hand check, one piece (Personal-Org-Operating-Model #49) is labelled with one plan, but its own work also drafted and reviewed the next plan, and a question asking you to approve that next plan was filed while it was under way. The two Critics who read it disagreed: one read it as about later work, the other as about this piece. As the rules stand, a disagreement counts against the piece, so #49 counts as needing you, and the hand check's figure is 14 of 33. Under the first reading it would be 13 of 33. The same kind of case will come up in the live count. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the hand check's figure for #49 rests on this, and the live count needs one rule for it rather than a case-by-case reading (research/Q-018-memo-5.md; reviews/Q-018-later-work/_critic-check-verbatim.md, rows 60 and 61).
OPTIONS:
  A. Yes: a plan the piece's own work wrote is work the piece carries, whatever its label says.
     Gain: matches how a plan the piece is labelled with is read; #49 stays as needing you (14 of 33).
     Cost: a piece that drafts the next plan as a side task takes on that plan's approval questions.
  B. No: only the plans the piece is labelled with are its own; approving any other plan is later work.
     Gain: one plain test, read from the labels alone.
     Cost: #49 drops to not needing you (13 of 33), and a piece's own drafting of a plan is never counted against it.
  C. Keep it as now: where the readers differ, it counts against the piece and is listed for you each time.
     Gain: no new rule.
     Cost: the result rests on whether two readers happen to agree, case by case.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with fair confidence (the Chief of Staff's judgement): the plan's rule already reads a spec "whose work the piece carries" as about the piece, and drafting and reviewing a plan is carrying its work; it also leaves today's figure as it is. What would change it: wanting a test read from labels alone (then B).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the second Critic of the later-work codes raised it; the step 5 Critic said #49's result hangs on it (reviews/Q-018-recode-5/_critic-verbatim.md, finding 12).
