# Ruling needed: the fair test's terms, settled before its plan freezes

Milestone: Design-6

The fair test's plan leaves eight terms for you to settle at the Build gate, before it freezes; each changes what the test does or what you are agreeing to. Two touch your value target's wording, which only you can change. One, letting copies of private repositories' work into public Model-Lab, could not be undone, because published history stays published. Each term has a recommendation; you may answer some and leave others for later, but the plan cannot freeze until every one is answered.

WHY: the plan's open questions must be ruled before the freeze, and the value target and anything irreversible are yours alone (specs/S-001.md, "Open questions for the owner"; Personal-Org-Operating-Model OPERATING_MODEL.md §2).
OPTIONS:
  1. The value target and M2 still say "on the same past tasks", which the test cannot use. A (recommended): reword them to "a paired test on new work, each piece done or checked both with and without the change from the same starting point over the same period", as the plan proposes, and restate the "about 300 runs" line with the plan's figures. B: keep the words; the test as planned would then not meet its own target's wording.
  2. Collecting the kinds. A (recommended): the next kind's pieces start collecting while the previous kind is judged and analysed, still analysed one kind after another: about 32 to 64 weeks for the full test instead of 60 to 121. B: strictly one kind after another. C: all three at once.
  3. A time limit. A (recommended): no fixed limit; each kind has review points 16 weeks after it opens and at each token checkpoint, where you may stop it on counts alone. B: a firm limit you name, at a stated cost in what the test can show.
  4. Recording each piece at dispatch. Plans and built work can only enter if each piece's brief, start commit and model are recorded when it is dispatched; today none is. A (recommended): the Chief of Staff records them for the Service Desk's and Model-Lab's work until the brief-and-model record (M3) is built, and Model-Lab's brief records gain a start-commit field. B: no recording; only research can enter.
  5. The judges. A (recommended): judges stay within your AI plan (Fable 5.1), with the limit that every judge comes from the same provider as the work. B: override your spend ceiling for an outside-provider judge on your sample cards only.
  6. Research's false-pass tolerance. A (recommended): accept the plan's tolerance of no more than one extra false pass. B: a stricter one you name.
  7. Judging sessions. A (recommended): one judging session per kind, each judge a helper inside it; you start 9 sessions for the full test. B: one session per judging: about 336 sessions for you to start for the full test, or fewer if the judging session starts them with tools no record has tested.
  8. Copies of private repositories' work. Each piece's start tree is copied into public Model-Lab for judging. A (recommended): only pieces from public repositories enter (today the Service Desk and Model-Lab); the headline weeks do not change. B: pieces from the private operating-model repository enter too, which publishes that repository's tree at each piece's start, for good.
RECOMMENDATION: A for each, with medium confidence (the Chief of Staff's judgement, from the plan's own proposals where it makes them): each keeps the test as the plan was reviewed, and 8A keeps the one irreversible step off the table. What would change them: for 2, wanting the strict reading of "incrementally"; for 7, wanting the plainer reading of your earlier ruling; for 8, wanting the operating model's own work tested too.
