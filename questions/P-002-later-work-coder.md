# Ruling needed: who decides whether a question asks for later work, in the past count and in the live count

Milestone: M8

Your answer that a question asking for later work counts on that later work needs someone to read each question and say which it is. The plan gave that to the Researcher. But the Researcher is not allowed to see your decision records, so that your leanings cannot steer its count. The live count is a script that reads no text, so nothing there can apply your answer yet. The plan also does not say which figure decides when a record does not say either way. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the re-count for your answer on later work cannot start until someone is allowed to code it, and the live count cannot apply it at all; the plan does not change until you choose (specs/S-002.md, ruling 11; reviews/S-002-rulings/critic.md, findings 1 and 2).
OPTIONS:
  A. The Critic codes it in the past count, from the records it may already read, and a second fresh Critic checks every code. In the live count, the Chief of Staff codes it in the touch log when each touch is logged. Where a record does not say, the touch counts on the piece under way and is listed for you.
     Gain: no rule on what each role may see changes; every code is checked; the starting figure is never lower than the records support.
     Cost: the Critic codes as well as checks, so a second Critic session is needed; "not said" counts against the piece.
  B. As A, but a record that does not say is left out of the figure and listed for you.
     Gain: the figure holds only touches known to be on this piece.
     Cost: the figure can undercount, and you settle the list by hand.
  C. Open these records to the Researcher in a pack for this task only, which you approve, and keep the live count as in A.
     Gain: the coder of the memo stays the Researcher.
     Cost: the Researcher sees your rulings and leanings, which the rule that keeps them out exists to prevent.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with fair confidence (the Critic's options; the Chief of Staff's judgement): it keeps the Researcher blind, checks every code, and errs toward counting you as needed rather than missing a touch. What would change it: wanting the starting figure to hold only certain touches (then B).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic's check of the rulings written in raised it (reviews/S-002-rulings/_critic-verbatim.md, findings 1, 2 and 8).
RISK CLASS: R0, a question only   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the hand check's touch count and the live count's touch log
DEFAULT: none: the counting rules change only on your answer   TIMEOUT: none
