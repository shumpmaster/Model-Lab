# Ruling needed: how the fair test's plan goes on after its fourth review failed

Milestone: M2, Design-5, Design-6

The plan for the fair test passed its review at the third round, but the last check before Design closes, which looks at whether the parts fit together, failed it on four points. All four are about how the plan connects to the tools that start helpers and hand them their reading. The first is that the tools cannot yet give the blind Check author its own reading list. The second is that nowhere says where the hidden marking sheets are kept or how they are fingerprinted. The third is that the Reviewer who analyses the results cannot open the records it needs. The fourth is that the plan's list of hidden files would also hide the Check author's own task sheet. The independent trace found one cause behind all four: no brief asked anyone to check the plan against those tools. It found nothing wrong with the test's measures, size or options (triage/P-001-S-001-round-4.md). The rules stop work here: no further review round until you choose.

WHY: the plan cannot freeze, and the Build gate cannot be put to you, until it passes; a fourth round is allowed only on your choice (Personal-Org-Operating-Model AGENTS.md, "Plan stop rule"; OPERATING_MODEL.md §4, "Stop rule").
OPTIONS:
  A. One focused confirmation review — the plan's writer fixes the four named points, plus three smaller ones in the same files, and one review checks only those, this time with the tools' files in hand. Cost: about two helper sessions, plus a rule change you would approve: the tools learn to give the blind Check author its own list, and the Reviewer gets the records it needs. If that review fails, it comes back to you the same way. It rules out nothing.
  B. Re-specify — the plan is rewritten and reviewed again from round one. Cost: several sessions and rounds, redoing parts that passed three rounds. It rules out nothing, but delays the Build gate most.
  C. Re-scope — narrow the plan, for example drop the blind held-back checks so your sample of judgements is the only outside check. Cost: a weaker test; the plan would need a new draft for the narrowed part. Only needed if you do not want the rule change in A.
  D. Drop — the fair test is not run. Cost: no result on whether any change helps; the project would go to you for a re-aim or close. Nothing found here threatens the test's worth.
RECOMMENDATION: A — the four points are about connections, not the design; Triage found the measures, size and options sound, and A fixes exactly what failed (triage/P-001-S-001-round-4.md).
