# Ruling needed: whether a question that asks for later work counts as stepping in on the piece under way

Milestone: M8

Some questions are put to you while a piece is under way and are about that piece's subject, but ask for changes after it lands. One example is the questions filed during Service-Desk #230. Some pieces also file question cards about other work, such as Service-Desk #237's five about a later design picture. The counting rules today count all of these as you stepping in on the piece, because their records name it. Your choice decides whether they count on this piece or on the later work. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the starting count of how often pieces need you rests on what a touch is; the plan does not change until you choose (specs/S-002.md, open question 11).
OPTIONS:
  A. Yes, it counts on the piece under way whenever its record names the piece, as the rules read today.
     Gain: simple, and it never misses a question asked while the piece ran.
     Cost: it counts questions the piece did not need answered, so the starting figure looks worse than the work was.
  B. No: it is a question about the later work, and counts on that work if it becomes a piece of its own.
     Gain: a touch means this piece needed you, which is what the value target measures.
     Cost: the coder must judge from the record whether a question asks for later work; where the record does not say, it is listed for you rather than guessed.
RECOMMENDATION: B, with moderate confidence (the Chief of Staff's judgement): the value target is the share of pieces that needed you before merging, and a question about later work was not needed by this one. What would change it: wanting no judgement in the count at all (then A, with each such record listed).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic raised it at step 5 and again in its re-check of the corrected rules (reviews/Q-018/_critic-verbatim.md, open questions; reviews/Q-018-rules/_critic-verbatim.md, finding 5).
RISK CLASS: R0, a question only   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the hand check's touch count and the starting figure; every trial's count of touches
DEFAULT: none: the counting rules change only on your answer (specs/S-002.md, open question 11)   TIMEOUT: none
