# Ruling needed: Who may change an agent's standing instructions?

Milestone: Discovery-3
Asked in session: session_01QBwHGJH9m3A4Ray7WpHzBo

WHY: Each agent works from a standing instruction file (its role) plus a short brief per task. Changes to the brief templates already need your approval as rule changes; the rulebook does not say whether changes to the role files do. The answer sets how fast instructions can be improved and how much of your time it takes.
OPTIONS:
  A. You approve every change — each change reaches you as a short card with its evidence; slowest, most of your time, nothing changes without you.
  B. You set limits, the team changes within them — for example, a change that passed its registered test and touches only wording may land without you, reported in the brief; anything changing what an agent may see, do or decide still comes to you. Faster; your time goes on setting the limits once.
  C. Decide later — Discovery maps the options and the Design gate brings a concrete proposal.
RECOMMENDATION: C — what the limits should be depends on what the research finds; deciding now would guess.
RISK CLASS: R1   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: how instruction changes are approved across the model's projects
DEFAULT: C   TIMEOUT: 168
