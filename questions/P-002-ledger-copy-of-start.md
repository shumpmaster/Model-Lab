# Ruling needed: whether a ledger copy of the ruling that started a piece counts as a touch

Milestone: M8

You ruled that a ledger entry quoting you counts once, skipped when an answer already counted on the piece holds the same words. The ruling that starts a piece is not counted as a touch, so a ledger copy of that starting ruling is never skipped and now counts. On two past pieces this changes the result: one goes from not needing you to needing you, and another from one touch to two. Your words were "so that one answer counts once", which can be read either way. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the re-count needs one reading before it runs; the plan does not change until you choose (specs/S-002.md, ruling 15; reviews/S-002-rulings/critic.md, finding 3).
OPTIONS:
  A. Count the copy, as the rules now read.
     Gain: no new skip test.
     Cost: a piece's starting ruling, which is not a touch, counts as one through its ledger copy, so pieces look as if they needed you when they did not.
  B. Also skip an entry when the piece's starting ruling holds the same words: the entry is that ruling.
     Gain: one answer counts once and the starting ruling counts nowhere, as for every other record.
     Cost: one more same-words check.
RECOMMENDATION: B, with fair confidence (the Chief of Staff's judgement): the ledger entry records the same ruling, and the rules already say a starting ruling is not a touch. What would change it: wanting the ledger read as its own record whatever it repeats (then A).
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic's check of the rulings written in raised it (reviews/S-002-rulings/_critic-verbatim.md, finding 3); the Definer raised it first (docs/briefs/314-definer.md, its return's open question 1).
RISK CLASS: R0, a question only   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the hand check's touch count, in Personal-Org-Operating-Model
DEFAULT: none: the counting rules change only on your answer   TIMEOUT: none
