# Ruling needed: the plan's re-review after today's changes failed on one point

Milestone: M9

Today's changes to the trial plan, which wrote in your touch-log choice, passed their own checks. The plan review then re-ran on everything they changed and failed on one point. A small clarification of when a piece's counting starts, written for the hand check of past work, also reaches trial pieces. There it would let a piece's counting start before it was entered and its side drawn. Whoever hands out the work can see which side comes next, so they could fold a new trial piece into a pull request that already holds a question you answered, and so place a piece certain to count as needing you on a side they choose. The reviewer says one clause closes it. The plan is past its last allowed round, so the stop rule sends the next step to you. Nothing is frozen, so nothing here is irreversible.

WHY: the plan cannot be frozen with a route to steer a trial, and the stop rule leaves the next step to you (reviews/S-002/plan.md, "Re-run on the changed lines, before the freeze", finding 1).
OPTIONS:
  A. One focused fix, then one check.
     The writer adds the clause: on a trial piece, counting starts at the entered piece's own start, whatever other work its commits name, and the reviewer lists every trial piece whose commits name other work. The writer also fixes the review's six smaller points or states them as limits. One check by the Critic covers only the changed lines; if it fails, it comes back to you.
     Gain: closes the route with one rule, and checks only what changed.
     Cost: one more change and one more check, which can itself fail.
     Undo: nothing is frozen until the check passes.
  B. Undo the start-time clarification and keep the plan as the earlier checks passed it.
     The clarification is taken back; the hand check's figures do not change, since no counted piece turns on it.
     Gain: no new rule and no further check of it.
     Cost: the rules again say nothing about a piece that carries two pieces of work, which the reviewer would then decide when judging a trial, after the sides are known.
     Undo: the clarification can be made later, with a check of its own.
  C. Re-specify how a trial piece's counting window is set, as a new draft.
     The writer redrafts the window rules and the Critic reviews them from round one, with three rounds again.
     Gain: a fresh look at a part that keeps meeting edge cases.
     Cost: the most time and checking; the cards and the freeze wait on it.
     Undo: the plan as checked so far is kept until the new draft passes.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with fair confidence (the Chief of Staff's judgement): the reviewer found nothing else that blocks, and the fix is one clause in a rule that already exists. What would change it: wanting no further check before the scenario card (then B).
