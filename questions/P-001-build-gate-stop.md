# Ruling needed: how the two Build-gate cards go on after failing their third check

Milestone: Design-6

The two cards you will rule the Build gate with, one asking you to move into Build and size the test and one settling the test's eleven terms, failed their independent check for the third time, the last allowed, so they have not come to you. An independent trace of the three checks found that of the last two faults one was a misreading and the other a one-line fix, and that a third point, rated minor, was a fault in the plan itself: it tied its shortest time, about 32 to 64 weeks, to an order of collecting that cannot give it. The plan's writer has corrected that, and two smaller slips the trace found in the plan about your own part; each correction passed its check. The trace's root cause is that the cards copy too much of a long plan, so each round's fixes added new claims to check. You choose how the cards go on; nothing here cannot be undone, and the gate itself still comes to you either way.

WHY: the cards cannot come to you without passing their check, and accepting them despite a failed check is never an option; the choice of how they go on is yours, since no grant lets anyone choose for you (Personal-Org-Operating-Model OPERATING_MODEL.md §4 "Stop rule" and §2 "Delegation grants"; docs/LEDGER.md; triage/P-001-build-gate-round-3.md, "Who rules").
OPTIONS:
  A. One focused check of named fixes — What happens: the Chief of Staff changes only the lines the trace names: where the cards say a failure "comes to you first", they name the four checks of the running that put a question to you, and say the wake messages simply fall to you; each size's chance says it is for built work, and the 100-piece size says plans stay at about 4 in 5; the term on the order of collecting is rewritten to the plan's corrected question, with each order's true weeks, and its recommendation looked at again, since the 32 to 64 weeks it rested on belong to a different order; and the term on your part adds the Orchestrator's wake fallback, as the corrected plan states it. One check in a fresh session reads only those lines and a comparison showing nothing else changed; any new claim fails it, and a failure comes back to you the same way (triage/P-001-build-gate-round-3.md, "One focused confirmation review"). Gain: the fewest changes, to cards whose other points passed three checks. Cost: the cards stay as long as they are, so the root cause stays; each earlier round's fixes added new faults. Rules out: nothing.
  B. Cut the cards down to the decisions — What happens: the Chief of Staff redrafts both cards to the decision-card form, two or three options and a short background: the gate card gives the sizes, hold, recycle and kill, and points to the plan's tables for runs, weeks and your part instead of copying them; the terms card argues only the terms that touch your earlier rulings, the value target or anything that cannot be undone, and lists the rest with the plan's proposed answer for you to accept or change. They are checked as new cards, from the first round (triage/P-001-build-gate-round-3.md, "Re-scope"; OPERATING_MODEL.md §2 "Decision cards"). Gain: far fewer claims that can be wrong, which is the root cause. Cost: up to three more rounds of checks before the gate reaches you; you read the figures in the plan rather than on the cards. Rules out: nothing.
  C. Redraft both cards in full — What happens: both cards are written again from scratch at their present scope and checked from the first round (triage/P-001-build-gate-round-3.md, "Re-specify"). Gain: a fresh start. Cost: it rebuilds the same long text that failed three times, and reopens points that passed. Rules out: nothing.
  Dropping the cards is not offered: the gate and the plan's freeze need your answers to the plan's open questions (specs/S-001.md, "Open questions for the owner" and "Needed outside this spec's lane"); ending the test is the gate's own "kill", which stays on the gate card.
RECOMMENDATION: A, with medium confidence (the trace's reading of the evidence, and the Chief of Staff's): the two remaining faults are one-line fixes, and the one figure that needed the plan is now corrected there. What would change it: a focused check that again finds new faults, or preferring the cure for the root cause over speed (then B). The rules limit plan and build checks to three rounds and name no limit for cards; the last session applied the limit to the cards, as you asked this one to, and if you read the rule as not covering cards, a plain fourth round is also open to you (triage/P-001-build-gate-round-3.md, "The stop rule's options").
WHO HAS CHECKED IT: the Critic, on a different AI model from the writer (record: reviews/P-001-build-gate-stop/critic.md).
