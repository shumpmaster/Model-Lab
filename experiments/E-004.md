**What this file is.** The fixed plan of every trial of a screened method. It is general: it names no method. Each trial copies it into a trial record of its own, `experiments/E-004/trials/<trial id>.md`, fills the blanks this file marks *per trial*, and freezes that record before its first piece is drawn (T27: the entry card "carries the trial's plan, which is then fixed"). Nothing in a trial record may change a rule of this file; a trial that needs a different rule is not a trial under E-004, and its own design and odds come to the owner first (T30; U5's fallback, docs/projects/P-002.md section 7).

**One choice is left to the owner before this file freezes:** the line at two looks (P18), version (a) or (b) below. The owner left it open (decisions/questions/P-002-design-gate.md, Part 7, "Decide later on checked figures (Recommended)") and rules it on a card of its own, filed by the Chief of Staff, once someone other than this file's writer has run experiments/E-004/line.py (the same record, "How it is carried out", P18). This file works with either; it does not choose or recommend.

Every ruled term below is given in its ruled words with its number in docs/gates/P-002/trial-terms.md. Where this file adds a detail the terms do not settle, it says "this plan's reading" or "this plan's design", for the Critic and the owner's scenario card.

## Who does what
- **The Chief of Staff** writes each entry card and results card (docs/projects/P-002.md section 4), keeps the queue (P15), records each piece's entry in the trial's log before its draw, logs by hand every question put to the owner in conversation (T29), and checks, before each entry card is put, that the trial keeps clear of the prompt engine (section 4, "Keeping clear of the prompt engine's work"; P12).
- **The Definer** writes each trial record from this file and the approved entry card's method, and sets its frozen line before the first draw.
- **The Critic** checks each entry card before it goes to the owner, including how the method is switched on and off piece by piece (U5) and the P12 check; and gives its view on each results card (T28).
- **The Builder** builds the count and the trial records' scripts (docs/projects/P-002.md section 4), runs each trial as registered (M13): the draw of each piece, recorded before work on it starts, and the trial's log.
- **The Reviewer**, which did not run the trial, works out each result against the line set in its trial record (T29); the Orchestrator records its verdict verbatim. It reads the trial's records through a per-task pack (S-002, "Needed outside this spec's lane").
- **The owner** approves each entry card before anything is tried (T12), reads 5 pieces per look (P14), and approves or rejects putting the method into the operating model on its results card, on any result, passed or not (T13).
- **The agents doing the pieces** do them as usual. A trial adds no run to any piece: each piece is done once, with or without the method.

## Hypothesis
For one method and one kind of work: the method's pieces need the owner less often than the pieces without it, by more than chance at the line in the trial record, while its work holds up (T24). "Not passed" is an accepted result and comes to the owner like any other (T14).

## Method

### Step 0 — before the first trial (P20, P5)
No trial runs before the trial design (M9) is frozen, the starting count (M11) is on record and the owner has set the ceilings (P20; the ceilings are set: decisions/questions/P-002-design-gate.md, Parts 2 and 3). If the starting count shows fewer than 6 of 60 pieces needed the owner, the work stops and comes back to the owner (P5), and no trial starts.

### Step 1 — the entry card and the trial record (T12, T27, T31)
Each method comes to the owner first on a plain-English entry card: what the change would be, how it is done today, why, and its impact and risks (T12), with what the first assessment rests on (T27). The card also carries, from its trial record:
1. **The kind of work** the trial runs on, one of built work, plans or research (S-002, rule K2).
2. **How the method is switched on and off piece by piece** (U5; T30), in the form of Step 3: the exact text or step applied to a "with" piece, where it is put, who puts it, and how it is kept off the "without" pieces. The Critic checks this before the card goes to the owner. A method that cannot be switched this way is not tried under E-004; its card says so, and the owner rules whether to try it another way, whose design and odds would come to the owner first, or not at all (U5's fallback).
3. **Its expected length** (U3), from Step 2.
4. **The evidence it was let through on**: one source someone else has opened and confirmed (T8), its conditions read against ours (T9), and any outside code, prompt or skill checked before it runs, with no data out (T10), each with its record (S-002, M10).
5. **The guards beside the prompt engine** (Step 7): which kinds the prompt engine is testing or counting on the day the card is put, and whether the method could change how often work passes its first check.
6. **The line** (Deciding threshold), the hold-up checks, the stops and the short reads, as in this file.
7. **What it costs the owner**: this card, a results card (T31), 5 short reads per look (P14) and the method's own touches.

At most 3 entry cards wait on the owner at once; the next waits in a queue the desk shows (P15). Nothing is tried without the owner's approval (T12). Once the owner approves, the Definer sets the trial record's frozen line; only then is the first piece drawn. The trial's plan is then fixed (T27).

*Per trial:* trial id (`T-<n>`, numbered in order of approval), method, kind, the switch (Step 3), the expected length, the entry card's record, the line (copied from this file's frozen Deciding threshold), the date of the frozen line.

### Step 2 — each kind's expected length (U3)
The count (M11) runs from the start of this project's Build (the owner's ruling beside the prompt engine: "the count of touches may run from the start of this project's Build", docs/projects/P-002.md section 6, last bullet). From its first 4 full weeks, the Builder's script gives, for each kind, the number of pieces that would have been eligible (Step 4), dispatched per week, and from that pace:
- weeks to the first look: 60 pieces at that pace, plus 30 days (P1);
- weeks to the second look, if taken: 120 pieces at that pace, plus two waits of 30 days (this plan's reading of P1 with two looks: Step 5).

The same figures are given again from the latest 4 weeks in each status update (P17) and on each entry card. *This plan's design, for the owner's scenario card:* if, from the first 4 weeks, a kind would take more than 26 weeks to its first look, no entry card for that kind is put until the owner rules on U3's fallback for it: try fewer kinds, wait longer, or narrow the target (docs/projects/P-002.md section 7, U3). The figure 26 is half the 12 months to the target's check (T5), and is the owner's to change.

The record count gives the pace so far only as upper bounds (research/Q-017-memo.md §1, "By kind": in the 60 days to 9 November 2026, pull requests touching built work 124 across the three repositories, plans 150, research 55, counted under every kind they touched). The count's own first weeks replace them.

### Step 3 — switching a method on and off piece by piece (U5)
A method is applied only through what is handed to the agent doing one piece: its brief, or a file named in its brief that no other piece's brief names. The entry card and the trial record state, for the method:
1. **On:** the exact text added to a "with" piece's brief (a fenced block, applied as its own paragraph after the brief's task statement), or the exact step, file or setting named in the brief, and who adds it: the session that writes the brief (the Orchestrator or the Chief of Staff), at dispatch, after the draw.
2. **Off:** the "without" piece's brief is written as it would be with no trial. The method's text is in no role file, no AGENTS.md, no template, no pack and no shared file that a "without" piece's agent reads; a method that must live in any of those cannot be switched per piece (T30).
3. **Leak check:** a marker the method's text or step carries (a phrase of at least 5 words that appears nowhere in the repositories at the trial record's frozen commit). At each look, the Reviewer searches every "without" piece's brief and merged files for it; a hit is a leak, listed with the piece. Leaks are reported on the results card; a leak is not a reason to drop the piece (Exclusions).
4. **Removal:** stopping the method is not adding it; nothing has to be undone outside the "with" pieces, whose merged work stays and can be undone as any work can (T26).

The draw is recorded in the log, and the brief carries no label of the trial or the arm beyond the method itself; the agents and the Chief of Staff can still know which pieces carry the method (T29: "only the owner's sample is unlabelled").

### Step 4 — which pieces enter, and the shuffled draws (T23)
- **Eligible:** a new piece of the trial's kind (S-002, rule K2, decided from its brief at dispatch) in any of the three repositories, dispatched after the trial record's frozen line is set, whose brief is recorded at dispatch, and which is not a record of this trial or of the prompt engine's test (its sheets, exports, verdicts or cards).
- **Entry:** before the brief is written, the Chief of Staff (or the session dispatching, which tells it) records the piece's entry in the trial's log: trial id, entry number, repository, brief number, kind, the dispatch time and the model the doer runs on (S-002, J2). The entry is committed before the draw.
- **The draw:** "each new piece of one kind is assigned, before work on it starts, to 'with the method' or 'without', by shuffled draws, so each side gets 30, in the same weeks" (T23). This plan's design of the shuffle: the look's 60 places, 30 "with" and 30 "without", are drawn one at a time without replacement, as from a shuffled deck: piece *i* is "with" with chance (with places left) / (places left), decided by the first 8 bytes of the SHA-256 of the text `<trial id>:<entry number>:<entry commit id>`, read as a number from 0 to 1. Each piece has exactly one entry and one draw; a second entry or draw for the same brief is void and listed. The draw is recorded in the log before the brief is written (T29).
- **The same weeks:** pieces are drawn in the order they are dispatched, so both sides fill over the same weeks. Because places are drawn without replacement, the last pieces of a look can be forced to one side; the Reviewer reports how many were.
- **The second look**, if taken (Step 5), draws its own 60 places, 30 each way, the same way.

### Step 5 — the two looks (T15, T16, P1)
"Two looks are planned per trial" (T15).
1. **Look 1** is judged 30 days after the last of its 60 pieces merges (P1, "Each trial is judged 30 days after its last piece", read at each look; this plan's reading). No new piece is drawn for the trial while look 1 waits.
2. At look 1: the method's pieces are **passed** if they needed the owner less often by more than chance at the line, and the work held up (T24; Deciding threshold); the result comes to the owner.
3. **The second look:** "if the first look leaned the right way (the method's pieces needed the owner less often) but did not pass, 30 more pieces each way are assigned, and all are judged together on a slightly stricter line, so that a useless method passes about as often as at one look (…); it is planned before the trial starts, so it is one trial, not a re-run" (T16). "Leaned the right way" means strictly fewer of the method's 30 pieces than of the other 30 needed the owner (a tie does not go on; this plan's reading, the same as the odds scripts, intake/P-002-value-target-odds-2026-11-10.md, twolook.py, `x<y`).
4. Look 1 does not go on to the second look if its hold-up checks fail (P6, Step 6).
5. **Look 2** is judged 30 days after the last of its 60 new pieces merges, on all 120 pieces, 60 each way, at the same line as look 1.
6. Look 1 with no lean the right way, or look 2 not passed, is **not passed**; nothing is dropped automatically, and every result comes to the owner (T14).

### Step 6 — the hold-up checks and the short reads (T25, P14, P6)
- **Holds up:** "the method's pieces do no worse, by more than chance at 1 in 10, at passing their first independent check and at not being reopened for a fault within 30 days; a reopening is marked by the reviewer of the later change, not the piece's maker" (T25). Each is a one-sided Fisher's exact test in the harm direction at 0.10 (the method's pieces pass less often, or are reopened more often), on the look's pieces (30 each way at look 1, 60 each way at look 2). A piece with no independent check is left out of the first measure only, and listed (S-002, rule C1). The work holds up when neither test clears 0.10.
- **Short reads (P14):** "at each look the owner reads 5 of the trial's pieces, chosen at random and unlabelled, 10 for a trial that takes its second look; one the owner would not have accepted counts as reopened" (docs/projects/P-002.md section 6, last bullet). This plan's design: at look 1, 5 drawn from its 60 pieces; at look 2, 5 drawn from its 60 new pieces; each draw by the order of the SHA-256 of `<trial id>:read:<look>:<entry number>`, lowest first, so the Chief of Staff does not choose. The Chief of Staff shows each as its merged work, with no brief, no arm and the method's marker removed where it appears in the brief only. A piece the owner would not have accepted counts as reopened in the hold-up check (P14). The short reads are read before the look is judged.
- **Stop at once (P6):** "a trial stops at once if its first look fails the hold-up checks, so the second look does not run and the result comes to the owner".

### Step 7 — stops, voids and the guards beside the prompt engine
- **P7:** "Stop a trial at once if it breaks work the owner relies on". No new piece is drawn; the result so far comes to the owner on a results card.
- **P8:** "a change of AI model during a trial voids it". The model each piece's doer runs on is in its entry (Step 4); a piece on a different model from the trial's first piece voids the trial, and the void comes to the owner. A change of AI model also triggers a re-check of the methods kept under the old one (P10).
- **P11:** one method per kind of work at a time. So at most three trials run at once, one per kind.
- **P12:** "no method that could change how often work passes its first check is tried on a kind of work while the prompt engine is testing that kind or counting its 30 real-use pieces". *Testing* is read as from the opening of that kind's comparison in the prompt engine's test (experiments/E-003.md) until its result is recorded; *counting* as from the day a change of the prompt engine enters the operating model until 30 pieces of that kind are counted under it (docs/PROJECT.md section 6, V2). A method is taken as one that could change how often work passes its first check unless its entry card shows why it could not and the Critic agrees. If the prompt engine opens that kind while such a trial runs, no new piece is drawn and the owner rules whether the trial waits or is voided (this plan's design).
- **P19:** "no method enters the operating model while the prompt engine is testing or counting the kind of work it changes, unless the owner rules otherwise for that method on its results card". The results card states the prompt engine's state for that kind on its day.
- **P20:** Step 0.
- **P4:** after the first five trials a summary comes to the owner, who rules whether to go on. No sixth entry card is put before that ruling.

### Step 8 — the results card (T13, T19, T28, T29, P16, T18)
After a trial is judged, or stopped, or voided, a results card in more detail, like a gate card, comes to the owner, who approves or rejects putting the method into the operating model on any result, passed or not (T13). It carries:
- the result the Reviewer worked out (T29), with the counts each way, the line, the p-value at each look, the hold-up checks, the short reads, leaks, forced draws, pieces left out and why, and the kind's pace;
- plainly, when the method was not passed and why, including when its work did worse (T19);
- the Critic's view and a recommendation (T28);
- the guards: the prompt engine's state for the kind (P19);
- if the owner may approve a method whose trial did not pass: that doing so goes against "Killed, not tuned", and the departure goes to the operating model as a proposal (T18).
Results cards are never held back, reach the owner within two weeks of a trial being judged, and never outnumber the entry cards approved (P16). A method the owner approves enters the operating model only through the model's own change process (T17), with a review date six months on and a way to remove it (P9).

## Measure
- **The deciding measure (T3, T24):** per look, the number of pieces each way that needed at least one owner touch before merging (S-002, rules T1 to T7).
- **The hold-up measures (T25):** per look, the number each way that passed their first independent check (S-002, rule C1), and the number each way reopened for a fault within 30 days of merging (S-002, rules R1 to R3, with the short reads, P14).
- **Reported, not deciding:** forced draws, leaks, pieces left out, the pace of the kind, the touches per piece, and the owner's short-read answers.

## Sample
30 pieces each way at look 1 and, if taken, 30 more each way at look 2 (T16, T23). "Each trial adds an entry card and a results card, and 5 short reads of unlabelled pieces per look, 10 for a trial that takes its second look" (docs/projects/P-002.md section 6, "Cost to the owner's time").

**How many of this project's agents run at once (docs/projects/P-002.md section 5, Tier).** A trial adds no agent to any piece (each piece is done once). This project's own agents, the sessions and helpers started for its work (reading rounds, research, checks, analysis, cards and the count), run at most two at once: the session that starts a helper, and one helper. So no part of this design needs three or more of this project's agents at once, and no tier question is needed. A change that would need three or more goes to the owner as a tier question first.

## Exclusions
- **Withdrawn:** a drawn piece that does not merge, with no owner touch, because its work was no longer needed is left out, listed with its side; its place goes back into the look's draw, so each side still reaches 30 merged pieces. The results card reports withdrawals by side.
- **Closed by the owner:** a drawn piece the owner closes or sends back for good stays in its side, as a piece that needed the owner (a send-back, S-002, rule T3).
- **Void entries:** a second entry or draw for the same brief (Step 4).
- **No first check:** left out of the first hold-up measure only (Step 6).
- **Not excluded:** a leaked piece (Step 3), a piece with an incomplete record (it is completed from the records or the touch log, and a gap is listed), a piece of an agent that knew its side (T29).

  - **(b) a line held at most 1 in 10 at every starting share: 0.093** (any line from 0.09227 up to, not including, 0.09359 decides the same). A method that does nothing passes 0.0677, 0.0882, 0.0963, 0.0908, 0.0842, 0.0908, 0.0963, 0.0882 and 0.0677 at starting shares of 1 in 10 to 9 in 10; at most 0.0982, near 0.668. - **Hold-up:** each check a one-sided Fisher's exact test in the harm direction at 0.10 (T25).
